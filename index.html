Standard primitives

AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 and HKDF-SHA-256. The preserved implementation authenticates its canonical header and rejects a wrong key or altered container.

Visible metadata

The filename, size, timestamp, algorithms and optional factor commitment remain visible. The encrypted content stays confidential only while your keys and device remain secure.
